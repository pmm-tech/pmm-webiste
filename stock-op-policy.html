    StockOp Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: January 23, 2026

    Effective Date: January 23, 2026

    1. Introduction

    StockOp ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application StockOp (the "App").

    By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

    2. Information We Collect

    2.1 Information You Provide

    • Account Credentials: Username and password for authentication
    • Server Configuration: Server URL for API connectivity
    • User Information: Information provided by your organization's backend system, including:
      • User ID and name
      • Outlet/store information
      • Stock opname session data

    2.2 Information Automatically Collected

    • Authentication Tokens: JWT (JSON Web Tokens) for secure API authentication
    • Device Information: Device type, operating system version (collected by Flutter framework)
    • Usage Data: App usage patterns and interactions (for app functionality)

    2.3 Permissions We Request

    Camera Permission: Required for barcode scanning functionality. We only access the camera when you actively use the scanning feature. Camera data is processed locally and not stored or transmitted.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use the collected information for the following purposes:

    • Authentication: To verify your identity and maintain secure sessions
    • App Functionality: To provide stock opname management features
    • API Communication: To communicate with your organization's backend server
    • Data Synchronization: To sync stock opname data with the server
    • Error Handling: To handle SSL certificate validation and user preferences

    4. Data Storage

    4.1 Local Storage

    We use secure storage mechanisms on your device to store:

    • Authentication Tokens: Stored using Flutter Secure Storage (encrypted)
    • User Information: Cached locally for offline access
    • Server Configuration: Stored securely for app connectivity
    • SSL Certificate Preferences: User preferences for SSL validation bypass

    All sensitive data is stored using industry-standard encryption methods.

    4.2 Server Storage

    Your organization's backend server stores:

    • User account information
    • Stock opname data and history
    • Inventory information
    • Transaction records

    We do not control or have access to data stored on your organization's server. Please refer to your organization's privacy policy for information about server-side data handling.

    5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

    5.1 With Your Organization

    All data collected by the App is shared with your organization's backend server for:

    • User authentication
    • Stock opname operations
    • Data synchronization
    • Business operations

    5.2 Third-Party Services

    • Flutter Framework: Uses standard Flutter services for app functionality
    • HTTP Client: Communicates with your organization's server via HTTPS
    • Secure Storage: Uses platform-native secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android)

    We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

    5.3 Legal Requirements

    We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

    6. Data Security

    We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

    • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted at rest and in transit
    • Secure Storage: Uses platform-native secure storage mechanisms
    • HTTPS Communication: All API communications use HTTPS encryption
    • Token-Based Authentication: Uses JWT tokens for secure authentication
    • SSL Certificate Validation: Enforces secure connections (with user consent for bypass)

    However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    7. Your Rights and Choices

    Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

    • Access: Request access to your personal information
    • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
    • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
    • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data
    • Opt-Out: Withdraw consent for data processing (may affect app functionality)

    To exercise these rights, please contact your organization's administrator or our support team.

    8. Data Retention

    • Local Device Data: Stored until you uninstall the app or clear app data
    • Authentication Tokens: Stored until you log out or the token expires
    • Server Data: Retained according to your organization's data retention policies

    9. Children's Privacy

    Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

    10. International Data Transfers

    Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your country of residence. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to servers that may be located in different countries.

    11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

    • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
    • Updating the "Last Updated" date
    • Sending a notification (if significant changes)

    You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
